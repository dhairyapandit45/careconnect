1. Problem Statement & Market Friction

Urban homeowners struggle to find verified, predictable, and transparent home trade services:

Fragmented Discovery & Pricing

Customers deal with opaque verbal quotes, hidden charges on-site, and erratic contractor availability.

Security & Credential Deficit

Unvetted technicians entering private homes without identity, address, or trade certification screening.

Zero Operational Visibility

No milestone tracking between initial booking confirmation and physical arrival.

Absence of Arbitration

Botched or incomplete repairs leave customers without dispute mediation or refund guarantees.

2. Proposed Solution: CareConnect Platform

CareConnect bridges the gap with a unified, transparent digital marketplace and operational engine:

Role-Specific Workflows

5 distinct portals tailored to Customers, Providers, Support Agents, Operations Managers, and Admins.

Verified Onboarding

Document verification pipelines (Identity, Address, Certifications) required before dispatch.

Explainable AI Heuristics

Instant category suggestions and multi-factor provider matching with 100% offline reliability.

Verifiable Work Orders

Strict job state machines requiring digital completion photo evidence before invoice issuance.

Financial Precision

Integer-cent accounting eliminating floating-point rounding errors on platform commissions.

Formal Dispute Mediation

Integrated arbitration queues and refund adjudication protecting customer funds.

6. End-to-End Fulfillment Flow

From Service Request to Completed Review:
  1. Step 1: Customer submits request; AI classifies category with confidence score.
  2. Step 2: Verified providers view marketplace and submit formal price quotes.
  3. Step 3: Customer accepts quote $\to$ Booking confirmed $\to$ Job work order provisioned.
  4. Step 4: Provider updates status: ASSIGNED $\to$ EN_ROUTE $\to$ IN_PROGRESS.
  5. Step 5: Provider uploads photo evidence and marks job COMPLETED.
  6. Step 6: Invoice generated in integer cents $\to$ Customer settles payment and submits 5★ review.

8. Verifiable Job State Machine

CareConnect eliminates contractor ambiguity with a rigorous operational state machine:

State Transitions
  • ASSIGNED: Booking accepted, technician notified.
  • EN_ROUTE: Travel initiated, ETA active.
  • IN_PROGRESS: Work started on premises.
  • COMPLETED: Work finished, evidence uploaded.
  • CANCELLED: Aborted with formal reason.
Digital Proof Verification

Before advancing to COMPLETED, the provider must upload:

  • Proof photo URL / document of the finished repair.
  • Detailed completion notes explaining parts replaced.
Enforced at Service Layer

9. Billing Architecture: Integer-Cent Precision

Why Not Standard Floats?
Standard IEEE 754 floating-point math causes rounding inaccuracies:
0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004

CareConnect stores all currency as integer cents (e.g. ₹1,200 is stored as 120000).
Platform Commission Split
  • Subtotal: Provider service fee.
  • Platform Fee: Configurable percentage commission (10%).
  • Tax: Applicable statutory levy.
  • Total Amount: Exact sum payable by customer.
  • Conversion to decimal happens strictly in display layers.

12. Explainable AI: Category Classification

Domain Keyword Taxonomy

Maps 100+ domain indicators (e.g. "p-trap", "mcb", "compressor", "sanitization") to service verticals.

Multi-Tier Scoring Matrix:
Name: +35 | Slug: +30 | Skills: +20 | Phrases: +18 | Tokens: +10
Confidence Formula & Fallback

Raw scores normalize into interval [0.50, 0.98]:

Confidence = 0.50 + min(score, 60) * 0.008
  • HIGH: ≥ 0.80 | MEDIUM: ≥ 0.60 | LOW: < 0.60
  • Deterministic Fallback: Zero-keyword input defaults to Home Maintenance (0.30 confidence) with transparent explanation.

  • CORS origin whitelist with credentials enabled.
  • Immutable audit logging records actor, action, IP, and timestamp.
  • Central error middleware suppresses stack traces in production.
